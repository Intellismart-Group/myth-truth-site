Camels store water in their humps

How sure are you?

MYTH

A camel's hump holds fat, not water. The fat is an energy reserve for times when food is scarce.

Source: San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance Library, Camels Fact Sheet: "Not used for water storage."
