Support

Questions, a claim you think is wrong, or something not working? Email pohm@intellismartgroup.com and we'll get back to you.

Common questions

When does the Daily come out?

A new Daily Challenge comes out every day at 12:00 UTC (4:00 AM Pacific in winter). Finish it before the next one comes out to keep your streak.

I think a claim is wrong.

Email us with the claim and what you found. Every claim shows its sources under the answer. If we correct a claim, you never lose points you already earned.

How do friend challenges work?

After you finish a Daily, tap Send a challenge and send the link or code to a friend. They open the link, or type the code in the Friends tab, and answer the same claims. Once they've answered them all, you both see how you compare. Links work for 7 days.

I got a new phone and lost my progress.

You play as a guest, so progress stays with the app on that phone. Deleting the app or changing phones starts you over.

How do I delete my data?

Open Report and tap Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy for what that removes.
