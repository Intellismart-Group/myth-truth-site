Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-11 · Last updated 2026-11-27

Myth / Truth is a game where you decide whether short claims are myth or truth and say how sure you are. You can play it in the iPhone app or on this website. It is made by Intellismart Global Staffing LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the game stores, why, and what you can do about it. It describes how the game actually works today; if that changes, we'll update this page first.

The short version

  • You play as a guest. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • We store your answers so the game can score them and show you your own Knowledge Report.
  • Nobody else sees your individual answers. Other players only ever see totals across at least 30 players.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics companies, and we don't sell data.
  • When you open the site, we count the visit by approximate city, the site that sent you, and phone or computer. We don't keep your internet address.
  • You can delete your data in the game at any time.

What we store

A guest identity

When you first start the game, our sign-in service gives your phone, or your browser if you play on the website, a random guest ID. It isn't linked to your name or Apple ID. If you delete the app, lose your phone, or clear this website's data from your browser, that guest ID and your progress can't be recovered. The app and the website each have their own guest ID, so progress in one doesn't carry over to the other.

Your game activity

For each claim you answer, we store: the claim, whether you picked Myth or Truth, how sure you said you were, whether you were right, the time between your two taps or clicks, and when you answered. We also store which Daily Challenges you finished and when, and any friend challenges you send or accept.

Technical information

When the game signs in, our sign-in service records the IP address and the device, browser and app details your phone or browser sends with the request, as part of keeping you signed in securely. Our hosting provider also keeps short-term technical logs of requests, which can include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting; on our current plan these are kept for about a day.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes, under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Visits to this website

When you open a page of this website, your browser sends one short message to our own stats server (proctorpilot.com): which page you opened, the site that sent you there (for example a search engine, or none if you typed the address), and whether this browser has been here before or is opening its first page that day. The server works out phone, tablet or computer from your browser's details and your approximate city from your internet address, then keeps only daily totals, such as how many visitors came from one city on a phone that day. Your internet address isn't stored, and the totals don't include your guest ID or anything else that identifies you. If this browser already has a guest ID, the page first asks our game server once a day whether it's one of the owner's own, so the owner's visits aren't counted; nothing is stored for that. The iPhone app doesn't send this.

Kept only on your phone or in your browser

Your answer to the 18+ question, the Explore category you picked and how often you skip a claim in Explore are saved on your phone, or in your browser when you play on the website, and never sent to us. The website also keeps the dates of this browser's first and latest visit; only whether a visit is the first one, or the first that day, goes with the visit count above.

How we use it

  • To run the game: score answers, keep your streak and points, build your Knowledge Report, and show Explore and Second Chance claims.
  • To show what other players believed on a claim, only after you've answered it and only once at least 30 players have answered. These are totals; no individual is shown.
  • To run friend challenges: the comparison is shown only to the two players in that challenge.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to limit abuse (for example, how fast answers can be sent).
  • To improve the game and its claims using totals and patterns across players.

Who else handles it

We use Supabase to host the game's database and guest sign-in, on servers in the United States. Supabase processes this data on our behalf. GitHub hosts this website, as described above. The website's visit totals are kept on our own server at proctorpilot.com, hosted by Cloudways and reached through Cloudflare; it looks up the approximate city itself, in DB-IP's free city database, so your internet address isn't sent anywhere else for that. Apple delivers the app through the App Store and TestFlight under its own privacy policy. We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your guest ID and game activity are kept until you delete them. Daily visit totals are kept for 2 years. Short-term request logs are kept as described above.

Deleting your data

In the app or on the website, open Report and tap Delete my data. This deletes that guest ID, its answers, streaks, points and Knowledge Report from our servers. If you play in both the app and the website, do it in each. Community totals are recalculated from the answers that remain. In friend challenges, your side is removed and the other player keeps their own answers. You can also email us to ask.

Age

Myth / Truth is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 is playing, contact us and we'll delete their data.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your data at any time, as above. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Email us and we'll help.

Changes

If we change what the game collects, we'll update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
