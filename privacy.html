MYTH / TRUTH

Privacy Policy

Effective DRAFT · Last updated 2026-11-11

Myth / Truth is a game where you decide whether short claims are myth or truth and say how sure you are. It is made by Intellismart Global Staffing LLC ("we"). This policy explains what the app stores, why, and what you can do about it. It describes how the app actually works today; if that changes, we'll update this page first.

The short version

  • You play as a guest. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.
  • We store your answers so the game can score them and show you your own Knowledge Report.
  • Nobody else sees your individual answers. Other players only ever see totals across at least 30 players.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics companies, and we don't sell data.
  • You can delete your data in the app at any time.

What we store

A guest identity

When you first open the app, our sign-in service gives your phone a random guest ID. It isn't linked to your name or Apple ID. If you delete the app or lose your phone, that guest ID and your progress can't be recovered.

Your game activity

For each claim you answer, we store: the claim, whether you picked Myth or Truth, how sure you said you were, whether you were right, the time between your two taps, and when you answered. We also store which Daily Challenges you finished and when, and any friend challenges you send or accept.

Technical information

When the app signs in, our sign-in service records the IP address and the device and app details your phone sends with the request, as part of keeping you signed in securely. Our hosting provider also keeps short-term technical logs of requests, which can include IP addresses, for security and troubleshooting; on our current plan these are kept for about a day.

Kept only on your phone

How often you skip a claim in Explore is saved on your phone only and never sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run the game: score answers, keep your streak and points, build your Knowledge Report, and show Explore and Second Chance claims.
  • To show what other players believed on a claim, only after you've answered it and only once at least 30 players have answered. These are totals; no individual is shown.
  • To run friend challenges: the comparison is shown only to the two players in that challenge.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to limit abuse (for example, how fast answers can be sent).
  • To improve the game and its claims using totals and patterns across players.

Who else handles it

We use Supabase to host the game's database and guest sign-in, on servers in the United States. Supabase processes this data on our behalf. Apple delivers the app through the App Store and TestFlight under its own privacy policy. We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your guest ID and game activity are kept until you delete them. Short-term request logs are kept as described above.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Report and tap Delete my data. This deletes your guest ID, your answers, streaks, points and Knowledge Report from our servers. Community totals are recalculated from the answers that remain. In friend challenges, your side is removed and the other player keeps their own answers. You can also email us to ask.

Age

Myth / Truth is for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you think someone under 18 is using the app, contact us and we'll delete their data.

Your choices and rights

You can delete your data at any time, as above. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Email us and we'll help.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
